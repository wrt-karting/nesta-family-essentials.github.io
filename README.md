# NESTA — Family Essentials

A complete, polished demo storefront for practical baby, parent and family products.

## What is included
- Responsive homepage with a dedicated hero layout.
- Separate Shop page with category filters and price sorting.
- Search overlay and persistent demo shopping bag using localStorage.
- 13 individual product photographs, one dedicated image per product.
- 9 category photographs.
- Local logo and favicon.
- About, Contact, Shipping & Returns, and Privacy pages.
- No external image URLs or remote product image dependencies.
- Images are square for products and consistent aspect ratios for category cards.

## Image quality standard
Every product photograph is locally packaged, centered, fully visible and presented with `object-fit: contain` so the product itself is not cropped by the card. Category photographs use their full dedicated card area without the extra inset that caused the previous small/incorrect presentation.
