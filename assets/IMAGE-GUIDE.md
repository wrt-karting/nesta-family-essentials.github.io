# NESTA Image Guide

## Product images
All 13 product files are unique and mapped one-to-one in `assets/products.json`. Product cards use a 1:1 frame and `object-fit: contain` to prevent product cropping.

## Category images
All 9 category files are dedicated photographs displayed edge-to-edge inside a consistent 1.54:1 frame.

## Logo
`assets/logo.jpg` is the approved NESTA logo supplied for the storefront.

### V5
- `logo.png`: transparent circular NESTA logo, no white square.
- `images/hero-family-real.jpg`: clean photographic hero region, separated from HTML copy.
